Challenge

Where they started

Enquiries arrived across calls, WhatsApp, email and walk-ins. Partners personally triaged every message, scheduling lived in three calendars, and the weekly report was assembled by hand — hours of senior time spent on work a system should do.

Strategy

The JSVita approach

One page, one goal: convert a visitor into a booked consultation. Behind it, an operations layer that routes, schedules and reports automatically — so the funnel feeds the firm, not the other way around.

Execution

What we built

Conversion landing page

Sharp copy, single action, sub-second load — engineered to book the call.

AI enquiry assistant

First-line responses and qualification, around the clock.

Workflow automation

Routing, scheduling and follow-ups handled without a human relay.

CRM integration & reporting

Every enquiry tracked; the weekly report writes itself.

Result

What it earned

A pipeline partners can actually audit — and more than ten senior hours back every week, with zero enquiries lost in the shuffle.
