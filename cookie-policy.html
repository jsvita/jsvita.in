Cookie Policy

Effective 2026 · jsvita.in

1. Overview

This policy explains how jsvita.in uses cookies and browser storage. We keep both to the minimum required to run the site — no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies are used.

2. What we use

  • Sign-in persistence — localStorage/sessionStorage keeps members signed in and preserves interface preferences. These are functional, first-party and never shared.
  • Security — standard technical measures (such as short-lived session storage) used to protect forms and accounts.
  • Analytics — if and when analytics is enabled, aggregate page metrics only; no advertising identifiers.

3. What we do not use

No advertising cookies, no cross-site trackers, no data brokers, no social-media pixels that build profiles across other websites.

4. Managing storage

You can clear or block cookies and site data at any time in your browser settings. The site remains fully browsable with storage disabled; only member sign-in and saved preferences are affected.

5. Changes & contact

Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Questions: support@jsvita.in. See also the Privacy Policy.
