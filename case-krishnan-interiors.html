Overview

The engagement at a glance

An interiors firm with an exceptional portfolio and near-zero search visibility. JSVita rebuilt the firm's web presence around technical SEO and content architecture — turning the site into its most consistent source of qualified enquiries.

Challenge

Where they started

Krishnan Interiors does work that photographs beautifully — and none of it was being found. High-intent searches went to weaker competitors with faster, better-structured sites. Referrals kept the firm busy, but growth depended entirely on word of mouth.

Research

What the research showed

Keyword research identified the exact high-intent queries paying clients type — 'interior designers in Chennai' plus room-specific and budget-specific variants. A technical audit then catalogued every crawl, speed and structure defect holding the old site back, and competitor SERPs were analysed to understand what page one actually rewarded.

Design Process

From brief to visual system

Information architecture

Service and location pages planned around real search demand, not guesswork.

Visual design

A portfolio-led aesthetic that lets the project photography do the selling.

Metadata & schema design

Title, description and structured-data templates drafted per page type.

Accessibility & vitals review

Contrast, focus states and layout stability checked against Core Web Vitals budgets.

Development Process

What we built

Technical SEO overhaul

Structure, crawlability and metadata rebuilt from the foundation up.

Schema systems

Rich structured data across services, projects and the organisation.

Core Web Vitals engineering

Speed budget per page — green vitals treated as a feature, not a hope.

Content architecture

Pages engineered around high-intent queries, not generic keywords.

Technology Stack

The stack behind it

  • Technical SEO framework
  • Schema.org structured data
  • Core Web Vitals engineering
  • Static-first architecture
  • Search Console & analytics integration

Outcome

What it earned

From invisible to first-page presence on target terms — and qualified enquiries that now arrive through the site, not just the phone book of old clients.

Before JSVita

  • Invisible for target searches
  • Slow, unstructured pages
  • Referral-only enquiries

After JSVita

  • Page-1 target rankings
  • Green vitals + structured data
  • 3× qualified enquiries

Lessons Learned

What it taught us

  • Rankings follow structure: crawlability, speed and schema are prerequisites, not extras.
  • Content written for real queries outperforms generic keyword pages several times over.
  • Design and SEO are not a trade-off — green vitals made the premium design load faster, not slower.
