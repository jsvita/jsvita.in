Before you ask

What is JSVita Web Solutions?

JSVita Web Solutions is the website development division of JSVita — business websites, startup websites, portfolio websites, landing pages, redesigns and maintenance, delivered to one premium standard.

What does a website cost?

Packages start at ₹1,299 (Starter) and the most popular Professional package is ₹4,999 — fixed, itemised proposals, no hourly meters. See the pricing section on the homepage.

Do you handle GST and company registration too?

Yes — through JSVita CA Services, the Chartered Accountant division of JSVita managed by CA Mahalakshmi. Many clients launch the website and registrations together with the Startup Launch Package.
